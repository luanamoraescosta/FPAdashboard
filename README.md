## Analysis of the Digital Communication of the Brazilian Parliamentary Agricultural Front

This project presents a data-driven analysis of the communication strategies used by the **Frente Parlamentar da Agropecuária (FPA)**, also known as the Brazilian Parliamentary Agricultural Front, on Instagram.


## About the Research

The **Frente Parlamentar da Agropecuária (FPA)** is one of the most influential parliamentary groups representing agricultural and agribusiness interests in Brazil.

This project investigates how the FPA uses social media — particularly Instagram — to communicate its political agenda, frame public debates, and present issues related to agriculture, land use, environmental policy, Indigenous territories, agrarian movements, and other topics connected to the Brazilian agricultural sector.

The analysis focuses on the **content and discourse of Instagram posts**, transforming the collected data into visualizations that allow patterns and recurring themes to be explored.

## Research Collaboration

This analysis was conducted in collaboration with **Bruna Távora**, whose research focuses on digital communication and political communication.
